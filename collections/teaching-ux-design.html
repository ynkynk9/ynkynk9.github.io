Research / Product Direction

3D Campus Navigation & Digital Services

A web-based 3D campus experience that brings spatial navigation, cultural communication and digital services into one accessible interface.

Project

Built with Web technologies and Three.js, the project combines an interactive campus panorama with building search, admissions information, student orientation, event discovery, registration and room booking. It explores how a spatial interface can make campus information easier to find while creating a more engaging way to experience campus culture.

Future Direction

The concept could evolve into a service platform for new-student onboarding, accessible wayfinding, campus cultural archives and everyday public services. With students contributing to research, interface design, development and content updates, it could also become a long-term project connecting digital-campus development with practice-based education.
